# Original hard crash effects

Three material-specific impacts synthesized from scratch without samples or external audio services. All are stereo 48 kHz, with both WAV and 192 kbps MP3 delivery.

- **impact-obstacle** (1.65 s): dense shell/carbon/plastic crunch, secondary structural collapse, dry fragments.
- **impact-gate** (1.85 s): inharmonic bent-metal clang, beating modes, frame rattles, scattered bright metal fragments.
- **impact-ground** (1.70 s): blunt strike, gritty friction scrape, two secondary ground thumps, tumbling debris.

Each contains an immediate broadband crack, driven 117/161/209 Hz body, 97→44 Hz sub drop, multi-band tearing and scattered tail events. Mastering uses nonlinear transient density control and independently verified MP3 headroom. No clipping, meaningful DC offset or cut-off tail. End samples are zero. Source script and full measurements are included.
