DJI AGRAS T25

A little smaller. A little smarter.

DJI AGRAS T25 redefines standards for compact agricultural drones. Light and nimble, DJI AGRAS T25 can easily be handled by one person. It can carry a spraying payload up to 20 kg[1] or a spreading payload up to 25 kg[1]. DJI AGRAS T25 is equipped with Front and Rear Phased Array Radars, a Binocular Vision System, and a high resolution FPV gimbal camera. DJI AGRAS T25 supports multiple missions, from surveying[2] to spraying and spreading and excels across a variety of terrains.

*The appearance of the product is subject to the actual product.
