Goggles

Can DJI Goggles 2 Battery be used to power other electronic devices?
No.
Can DJI Goggles 2 Battery supply power to DJI FPV Goggles V2?
Yes. But the included power cable (USB-C) is not compatible.
Can DJI Goggles 2 be powered by a third-party battery?
It is recommended to use the original DJI battery. If you would like to use a third-party battery, please ensure its voltage should be no more than 11.1 V in case that the drone is scorched.
Can I wear myopia glasses when using with DJI Goggles 2? Do the goggles support diopter adjustment?
DJI Goggles 2 support diopter adjustment, ranging from -8.0 to 2.0 D. Therefore, myopia glasses are not needed during usage.
Eyeglass frames are provided for free when you purchase DJI Goggles 2. If you need astigmatism correction or if the goggles’ diopter is unsuitable, you can purchase additional corrective lenses from an optical shop. Then, install the lenses to the eyeglass frames for usage.
Which aircraft can be used with DJI Goggles 2?
DJI Goggles 2 can only be used with DJI Avata from the date of product release. Please stay tuned to DJI’s official website for the new information on the goggles’ compatibility.
Can DJI Goggles 2 be used with a third-party antenna?
Yes. DJI Goggles 2 can be used with a third-party antenna that comes with an MMCX connector.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the screen recording after a microSD card is mounted?
Yes. If a microSD card is mounted, DJI Goggles 2 can support the screen recording with the on-screen display (OSD) information recorded.
Is there a built-in image transmission receiver in the DJI Goggles 2?
Yes, the DJI Goggles 2 are equipped with an image transmission receiver.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the 2.4 GHz frequency band?
Yes.
Can videos stored in a microSD card be copied or viewed after DJI Goggles 2 are connected to the computer?
No.
Can DJI Goggles 2 transfer audio in real time?
No. As the drone does not contain the feature of audio receiving, audio cannot be transferred in real time during flight. However, DJI Goggles 2 have a built-in speaker that can play audio from a stored or during screen casting.
What is the function of the 3.5 mm audio port on DJI Goggles 2?
The 3.5 mm audio port on DJI Goggles 2 can be used for playing audio from a video stored in a microSD card or during screen casting after a 3.5 mm headphone is connected to the port.
Can the OSD information shown on the DJI Goggles 2 interface be disabled?
No.
Can DJI Goggles 2 be connected to a simulator?
Yes, DJI Goggles 2 can be connected to the DJI Virtual Flight app.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support playing back a video?
Yes. DJI Goggles 2 can be used to play back videos stored in the microSD card, or to cast DLNA videos played on the mobile device.
Supported video format when playing back a video stored in the microSD card: MP4 and MOV
Do DJI Goggles 2 support a third-party device?
No.
Do DJI Goggles 2 have a 3.5 mm standard audio port?
Yes.
Can DJI Goggles 2 be used to play 3D videos?
No.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the Motion Control and Head Tracking?
Yes.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the analog image transmission?
No.
Can I use DJI Goggles 2 to view the Sphere Pano photos?
No.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the HDMI input/output?
No.
What languages do DJI Goggles 2 support? How do I change the current language?
Supported languages are as follows:
English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Russian.
The two methods below can help change the system language:
[Method 1]
Select the system language according to the on-screen instructions if you use the goggles for the first time.
[Method 2]
Swipe right from the left of the touch panel to open the goggles menu, and then go to Settings > About to select the system language.
Can DJI Goggles 2 be used with the DJI’s remote controller with a built-in screen?
Please stay tuned to DJI’s official website for the new information on the goggles' compatibility.
Can I use the DJI Goggles 2 album to view the files recorded in the aircraft?
Please insert the drone's microSD card into the goggles, then go to the album to preview files recorded by the drone.
How can DJI Goggles 2 be connected to a mobile device?
[Method 1] Use the USB-C OTG cable and the USB-C/Lightning cable to connect DJI Goggles 2 to a mobile device.
[Method 2] Use the USB-C to USB-C or USB-C to Lightning cable to connect DJI Goggles 2 to a mobile device.
[Note] Please use a USB-C cable that supports a standard protocol or an MFI-certified Lightning cable. Currently, DJI Goggles 2 cannot be used with any non-standard data cables including high speed 5A current purple core super-fast charging data cable.
What is the difference between DJI Goggles 2 and DJI FPV Goggles V2?
Compared to DJI FPV Goggles V2, DJI Goggles 2 have integrated more functions, and can be compatible with more DJI products such as the new-generation camera drones.
Take the function of Head Tracking for example, the drone and gimbal can be controlled through head movements, and the goggles can let the user have a next-level, immersive flight experience.
The DJI Goggles 2 also adopt DJI’s latest HD, ultra-low-latency image transmission system.
In addition, the goggles have been improved in many other aspects such as high-performance dual display, diopter adjustment, wearing comfort, compact design, and UI design.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the DJI Digital FPV System?
No.
Do DJI Goggles 2 support the real-time live view output (or live streaming)?
It is not currently supported.
What are the specifications of DJI Goggles 2’s screen display?
Resolution: 1920 × 1080
PPI: 4496 PPI
Max Brightness: 700 Nits (Note: Seven options from 100 to 700 nits can be set.)
Material: Micro-OLED (Silicon-based OLED)
What are the video resolution and frame rate for DJI Goggles 2?
Video Resolution: 1920 × 1080
Frame Rate: 50 fps
What are the precautions when I use the DJI Goggles 2?
If DJI Goggles 2 are used in a sunlit environment, please keep the lenses away from direct sunlight. Otherwise, it may cause irreversible damage to the optical components.
What is the weight of DJI Goggles 2?
It is approximately 290 g (with headband).

Drone

Is DJI Avata waterproof?
No.
What remote controller can be used with DJI Avata?
DJI Motion Controller and DJI FPV Remote Controller 2.
Can DJI Avata be compatible with other goggles?
The drone can only be used with the DJI Goggles 2 and DJI FPV Goggles V2.
Does DJI Avata have the auxiliary bottom light?
No.
Does DJI Avata have the internal storage?
Yes. Its internal storage is 20 GB.
Can DJI Avata sense and avoid the obstacles?
No. But the drone can automatically brake and hover in place in Normal/Sport mode when the downward vision systems are enabled.
What are the image size and format supported by DJI Avata?
Image Size: 4000 × 3000
Format: JPEG
What is the video format supported by DJI Avata?
MP4.
Does the DJI Avata camera support distortion correction?
Yes. Three options of distortion correction can be set: Standard, Wide-Angle, and Super Wide Angle.
Does DJI Avata support the real-time live view correction?
No. But video correction is available after a video is recorded.
What are the color profiles of DJI Avata?
It supports Standard and D-Cinelike color profiles.
Does DJI Avata support RockSteady EIS?
Yes. Three options can be set: Off, RockSteady, and HorizonSteady.
What is the sensor size of the DJI Avata camera?
It has a 1/1.7” CMOS sensor with the effective pixels of 48 MP.
Can I manually configure the camera parameters for the DJI Avata drone?
Yes. The ISO, shutter speed, EV, and white balance can be manually configured.
What is the maximum transmitter power (EIRP) of DJI Avata under each wireless certification standard?
FCC: < 33 dBm
CE: < 14 dBm
SRRC: < 30 dBm
Can I read the DJI Avata drone’s information after connecting the mobile device to the drone?
No. But after you connect the drone to the computer via the USB-C port, the information stored in the microSD card can be read, and the firmware can be updated using DJI Assistant 2.
How long does it take for the DJI Avata drone to search for satellites?
If the drone is used frequently within a short time and there is no signal interference, it takes about 20 seconds to search for satellites; and if it is not used frequently, it may take longer.
What is the image transmission range for DJI Avata?
10 km (FCC)
2 km (CE)
6 km (SRRC)
Does DJI Avata support third-party remote controllers and goggles?
No.
What intelligent feature does DJI Avata have?
Smart Return to Home (RTH).
Can the DJI Avata drone hover in place automatically?
The drone can hover in place automatically in Normal/Sport mode.
What should I be aware of when operating DJI Avata?
Under the current circumstance, if one single drone is being operated, please be aware of the items below:
1) Please do not fly in areas with interferences such as Wi-Fi signals.
2) Please do not fly the drone too far away or at a very low altitude.
3) Please make sure that the drone should fly in safe and unrestricted areas. For detailed regulations, please consult your local laws and regulations.
4) Please ensure the usage of the goggles should be in accordance with the local laws and regulations.
5) Before each flight, it is recommended to connect it to the mobile device to update the firmware and GEO zones, and register your flight activities according to the local laws and regulations.
What are the precautions when I use the DJI Avata drone outside my current country/region?
First, you should learn about the local radio frequency laws and regulations before you go.
Second, connect the drone to DJI Fly after you arrive at the area so that the app can automatically tune the system settings.
What should I be aware of before I take off or land the DJI Avata drone?
1) Before takeoff, please ensure the propellers have been mounted properly, the sensors have been calibrated successfully, and the batteries have been fully charged. Also, please ensure there are no obstacles around, such as trees, buildings, or pedestrians.
2) Before landing, please choose an area with a flat surface away from water, and ensure no obstacles are nearby. It is recommended to use the aircraft in Normal mode for landing.
How can I link the DJI Avata drone, DJI Goggles 2, and remote control device?
1. Power on the drone, goggles, and remote control device.
2. Press the link button on the goggles, and it beeps.
3. Press and hold the power button on the drone until its battery level LEDs blink in sequence.
4. After successful linking, the drone’s battery level LEDs turn solid, showing the actual battery level. Then, the goggles stop beeping, and image transmission can be displayed normally.
5. Press and hold the power button on the drone until the battery level LEDs blink in sequence.
6. Press and hold the power button on the remote control device until it starts to beep continually, and the battery level LEDs blink in sequence.
7. After successful linking, the battery level LEDs of both the drone and remote control device turn solid and display the battery level. Then, the remote control device stops beeping.

* For detailed operations, please refer to the user manual or watch the tutorial video on the DJI's official website.
How can I activate DJI Avata?
A brand new DJI Avata must be activated through the DJI Fly app.
First, please power on the drone, goggles, and remote control device, and ensure all the devices are linked.
Then, connect the USB-C port of the goggles to the mobile device, run the app, and activate the device by following the on-screen prompts. Note that an internet connection is required for activation.

* For detailed operations, please refer to the user manual or watch the tutorial video on the DJI's official website.
How can I update the firmware of DJI Avata?
[Method 1] Use DJI Fly to update the firmware.
1. Power on the drone, goggles, and remote control device, and ensure all these devices are linked.
2. Connect the USB-C port of the goggles to the mobile device, run the app, and update the firmware by following the on-screen prompts. Note that an internet connection is required for firmware update.
[Method 2] Use DJI Assistant 2 (Consumer Drone Series) to update the firmware.
1. Power on the device. Connect the USB-C port of the device to the computer using the USB-C cable.
2. Launch DJI Assistant 2 (Consumer Drones Series), and log in to your DJI account.
3. Click the icon of the device, and click the “Firmware Update” option on the left.
4. Select and confirm the firmware version.
5. The firmware will be downloaded and updated automatically.
6. The device will restart automatically after the firmware update is complete.

* For detailed operations, please refer to the user manual or watch the tutorial video on the DJI's official website.
Does DJI Avata support the function of Find My Drone?
Yes. Go to the menu of the goggles, and then go to Settings > Safety > Find My Drone.
It helps find the location of the drone on the ground by using the cached video in the goggles. If the drone still has power, turn on ESC beeping, and the beeping sound will lead you to locate the drone.
Can I fly the DJI Avata drone without wearing the goggles?
The goggles provide the first-person view, letting you “fly” in the pilot seat.
Without the goggles, you can only have the experience of operating a drone, instead of enjoying stunning visual effects.

Gimbal

How many axes does the DJI Avata gimbal have?
The DJI Avata drone is equipped with a single axis (tilt axis) gimbal stabilizer.
What is the maximum gimbal tilt angle of the DJI Avata drone?
Mechanical Range: -90° to 70°

How can I maintain the DJI Avata intelligent flight batteries?
1. Do not charge the battery immediately after flight as it may be too hot. Wait until it cools down to the room temperature.
2. The temperature range for charging is from 5 to 40°C, and the ideal range is from 22 to 28 °C as it will significantly prolong the battery life.
3. Store the battery in a cool place.
4. Before storage, charge the battery to 50-60%, and fully charge it at least once every three months in case that its battery cell will be malfunctioning due to over-discharge.
What is the charging logic of the DJI Avata battery charging hub?

What are the status LED indicator descriptions of the DJI Avata battery charging hub?
Turns solid yellow: The battery is not in place.
Pulses green: The battery is being charged.
Turns solid green: All batteries are fully charged.
Blinks yellow: The temperature of the battery is too high or too low (no further operation needed).
Turns solid red: The overcurrent, abnormal voltage or other battery error occurs (unplug and plug the batteries or charger to resume charging).
